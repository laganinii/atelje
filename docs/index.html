atelje

A creative desk for traces: notes, photo polaroids, links and sticky-note replies. Everything stays on your device.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions: github.com/laganinii/atelje/issues

FAQ

Do I need an account? No.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Atelje has no server and works offline.

How do I export my desk? Settings → Export desk (opens the iOS share sheet).

How do I delete everything? Settings → Sign out — erase data, or delete the app.
