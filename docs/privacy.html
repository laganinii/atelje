Atelje — Privacy Policy

Developer: laganinii · Effective date: 20 October 2026

Summary: Atelje does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Atelje has no backend and makes no network requests. We (the developer) never receive your notes, photos, links, name or roles.

Data stored on your device

The name you choose, your selected roles, the traces on your desk (notes, photos, links, replies) and your Inbox are saved locally on your device using the app's private storage, so they are there the next time you open the app. This data is not sent anywhere. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which are governed by Apple's terms.

Camera and photos

When you add a photo, iOS shows its own picker. If you choose "Take Photo", iOS asks for camera permission; the camera is used only to take that picture. Photos you add are resized and stored only inside the app on your device. Atelje does not read the rest of your photo library.

Export and sharing

"Izvezi" (Export) creates a JSON file of your desk and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). Atelje sends nothing by itself.

Example content

The desk starts with example traces from fictional collaborators (Iva, Noa, Marko, Lena, Dora) to show how the app works. They are not real people or accounts.

Children

Atelje collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Open Postavke (Settings) and tap "Odjava — obriši podatke" to erase everything Atelje stored on the device, or simply delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
